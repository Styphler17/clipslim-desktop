# ClipSlim Desktop

Fast, private video compression for Windows. Everything runs on your PC; your videos are never uploaded.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/Styphler17/clipslim-desktop/releases/latest)** (`ClipSlim-Desktop-Setup-<version>.exe`).
Installed copies update themselves automatically.

## What it does

- Compress big recordings (screen captures, phone and camera videos) to a fraction of their size.
- Drag and drop videos or whole folders; see the estimated size and time before you start.
- Trim the start and end, or remove the audio, per video.
- Choose the resolution (Original, 1080p, 720p, 480p) or a ready-made preset (sharing online, chat and email, screen recordings).
- Compare the original and the compressed video side by side.
- Keeps running at full speed in the background and keeps your PC awake until the batch is done.
