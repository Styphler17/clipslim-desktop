# Privacy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

ClipSlim Desktop compresses videos **on your computer**. Your videos, file names and settings never leave it.
There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## The only times ClipSlim goes online

| What | Sent to | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Update check (at start and every 6 hours) | GitHub | A normal download request (your IP address is visible to GitHub) | To find and download new versions |
| Activating or re-checking a Pro key bought online (about once a week) | Lemon Squeezy | Your licence key, and your computer's name to label the activation | To confirm the key is valid and count it against its 2 PCs |

Owner-issued keys (starting with `CS2-`) are checked offline and send nothing.

## Purchases

When you buy Pro, Lemon Squeezy processes your name, email address and payment details as merchant of record,
under [its privacy policy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy). We receive your name, email address and order
details from Lemon Squeezy to provide support and refunds, and keep them only as long as needed for that and for
accounting rules.

## On your computer

Settings, presets, history and the licence are stored in your Windows user profile. Uninstalling ClipSlim and
deleting `%APPDATA%\compressgpt-desktop-ui` removes them.
